Kanjidex concept

A redesign of Kanjr around one idea: you are out in the world catching kanji. Ink black, vermilion red and paper white, with gold reserved for burned kanji. The hanko seal stamp is the signature mark: every kanji you learn gets stamped into your dex. Below: palette, stage ramp, and four screens as they would look.

Design rules

  • Three colours do the work. Ink for structure and text, vermilion for the one thing to do next, paper for everything else. Gold appears only on burned kanji, so it stays special.
  • Outlines, not shadows. Cards have 2px ink borders with a hard offset shadow, like printed cards or stickers. No gradients, no glass.
  • The seal is the mark. A tilted red square stamp with the kanji inside is the logo, the favicon, the app icon, and the "caught" animation (a stamp thumps down with the correct-answer ding).
  • Words carry the adventure. Reviews are encounters, lessons are new sightings, the grid is the dex, the scanner is the catch screen, the level badge is trainer level. Wrong answers "slip away"; nothing is ever called a failure.
  • Typography. Zen Kaku Gothic New (black weight) for headings and numbers: chunky, friendly, and it sets kanji and Latin in the same voice. Manrope for interface text. Noto Sans JP for the kanji themselves, with the typeface-variety setting still rotating faces in encounters.
  • Dark theme flips paper to a deep ink brown and keeps vermilion; it reads like the same object at night, not a different app.
  • Nothing borrowed. No creature art, no capture-ball shapes, no copied type. The stamp, the ? silhouettes and the trainer card carry the feeling without any trademarked element.

What changes in the code

  • Tokens in style.css: new palette, stage ramp, fonts, border-and-offset-shadow card style. Most screens pick this up automatically because they already use the tokens.
  • Copy pass in app.js: the adventure vocabulary on Home, Reviews, Lessons, Grid, Scan and the toasts.
  • Grid: "?" silhouettes for unseen kanji with a reveal toggle; dex entry gains "caught at" when a kanji came from a scan (place and date, if you allow location).
  • Icon, favicon, manifest colours; the stamp animation on correct answers; Home trainer card layout.
  • Estimated effort: about a day for tokens, copy and Grid; a second day for the stamp animation, icon and trainer card.
