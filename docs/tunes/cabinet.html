Kanjr Tune Cabinet

Candidate music for the app, on the same four-channel Game Boy chip as before: two pulse waves, a triangle bass and a noise channel. Nothing here is installed yet. Play them, and tell me which ones to keep, which to rework and what is still missing. The current tunes are included in each section, dashed, so you can compare directly.

What I changed about how they are written

  • Every candidate leads with a hook in the first two bars, the way the celebration fanfare does, instead of wandering up and down a scale.
  • The harmony channel plays a countermelody or held chords rather than endless broken chords, so there is something to follow underneath.
  • The bass has a shape of its own: bouncing octaves, a walking line, or a chromatic climb.
  • Each one commits to a character, named on the card. If a name sounds wrong for its screen, that is useful to know too.
